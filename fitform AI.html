Two exercises. Done properly.

FitForm watches your joints through the camera and tells you what to fix while you are still moving.

Squat

  • Tracks your knee angle on every frame.
  • Counts a rep only when you reach 90° depth and stand back up fully.
  • Says "Squat lower" when a rep is too shallow, and does not count it.
  • Warns you if you go too deep (under 55°) to protect your knees.
  • Reminds you to keep your chest up if you lean too far forward.

Push-up

  • Tracks your elbow angle on every frame.
  • Counts a rep only when your elbows reach 90° and fully lock out on the way up.
  • Says "Lower your chest" when a rep is too shallow, and does not count it.
  • Warns you when your hips sag or lift out of line with your shoulders.

Automatic rep and set counting

No manual logging. Reps are grouped into sets, and a set closes on its own after you rest.

Live voice and visual cues

Spoken tips and a color-coded skeleton: green is good form, amber is not deep enough, red needs fixing.

Cadence and form score

See your reps per minute and the share of reps done with clean form.

Private by design

Video is processed in your browser and discarded. Nothing is uploaded or recorded.
